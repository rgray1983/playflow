# Operational app pass — 2026-10-21

Owner direction: complete the operating app and live dashboard before guided SaaS onboarding and subscription billing. The authorized scope is party checkout, booking communications, configurable forms/modules, reporting/payment corrections, and dashboard refinement.

## Implemented

- Shared party checkout retains cash/external payment recording, online Square cards, deposits, balances, split rules, and family extras. Its payment action area stays visible while details/entry scroll; smaller screens have details/payment tabs.
- iPad Square POS handoff reserves an existing checkout before launch, limits tender to cards, fixes location/amount, and uses an unpredictable server note/state. The return is never trusted as proof: retrieve the order, then the tender payment, and verify completed status, exact note, source, USD amount and location. An offline/missing/invalid response remains pending. A return can be retried in the same browser after refresh. No physical-reader test has been performed.
- Online deposit preflight requires the signed rental agreement before a charge. Staff confirmation, public confirmation links and workflow advancement require the deposit requirement and a signed hosting-parent rental agreement. Resolving the deposit/agreement automatically confirms an unexpired, non-overlapping hold. Staff can explicitly waive the deposit through the existing policy. No confirmation action charges a saved card. Workflow steps advance in order; cleanup/completion require settled party balances, and completion also requires guests checked out.
- Each tenant can publish liability/rental wording under Company Settings → Waivers. Publishing uses optimistic version checks and a tenant lock. New signatures validate the current business version under the same lock and save immutable wording, consent and per-child snapshots. Palmetto Playhouse keeps its existing wording until deliberately republished. New businesses never inherit it. Existing signed documents are not rewritten or automatically expired by publication.
- Company Settings → Modules persists switches for new admissions, parties, membership enrollments and retail sales. The services enforce the switches after idempotent replay checks. Existing records, membership usage, checkout, payment, history and corrections remain accessible. Plan entitlements and unused future modules are not presented as working switches.
- Company Settings → Communications shows durable, deduplicated hold/confirmation/reminder previews, recipients, private-host versus shareable-guest links, approval/cancel actions and delivery status. Confirmed status alone does not trigger emails without deposit/agreement evidence. Automatic queuing defaults off. Immediate delivery runs after successful staff/parent mutations and signed payment callbacks when configured; reminders have a secret-authenticated processor endpoint. Stale/cancelled messages are cancelled. Ambiguous retries use the same provider key within 23 hours, then require review. SENT means provider accepted, not verified inbox delivery.
- Reports shows business-local daily collections, original deposits counted once, completed refunds, net collections, separately selected Sandbox activity, unpaid operational balances, Square receipts and CSV of displayed payments. Aggregate totals are unbounded; display/export is bounded to 100 rows per source. Counter/party cash collections are separate from recognized revenue or bank settlement and exclude Square fees. Unpaid operational balances cover all saved test/live records and are explicitly labeled.
- Owner/manager Square refund requests reserve amounts under the business lock, prevent concurrent over-refunds, use the original merchant/environment/payment, preserve original collections, and retry safely with stable keys. Pending requests poll Square after an ID is known. Only verified completed refunds reduce reported net collections. Refunds do not automatically cancel bookings, reopen bills, revoke memberships or alter attendance.
- Dashboard displays live/recorded collections, completed refunds/net, separate Sandbox totals, today's unpaid parties, missing rental agreements/guest waiver counts, and pending payment/message alerts. Financial fields require collection permission. Existing attendance and schedule summaries remain tenant scoped.
